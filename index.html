Datasets

Every published proteome in which a subunit of the ER membrane protein complex (EMC) was knocked out or knocked down, collected in one place and re-analysed on common terms. Each value can be traced to its source file and the rule applied to it, membrane proteins can be shown on their own, and proteins from mouse, frog, yeast and trypanosome datasets are linked to their human orthologues where one exists.

Squares are EMC1 to EMC10, shaded by log2 change from dark blue (down) to orange (up); the ringed square is the subunit perturbed; dashed means not detected. Hover a square for its value. Where a paper reports no subunits, the status is inferred from the subunit perturbed and marked as such.

What coverage means

  • Full table: every quantified protein is published, so a protein that is absent was not detected, and a protein that is present but unchanged was measured and unchanged.
  • Called hits: only a selection is published (for example a top-20 list). A protein missing from the list is unknown, not unchanged.
  • Named proteins: the paper names proteins and their direction in the text or a figure, without values.
